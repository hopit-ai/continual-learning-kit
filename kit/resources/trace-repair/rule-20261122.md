# Declared overlap repair (owner, 22 Nov 2026)

Rule version: trace-repair-20261122.v1. Scope: TRACE gold/27b only.
S8 overlap adjudication is out of scope. Eval and test rows never change.

Apply the following rules to each audit_rows flagged train/held-out pair,
in this order. A drop removes only the released training row ID; retain IDs
and input order. A row is dropped if any pair requires its removal.

- R1: If reasons include source_group, drop the training row.
- R2: If the held-out row came from an exclusions population, rather than
  a TRACE task eval/test split, drop the training row.
- R3: If the training task is FOMC or MeetingBank, drop the training row.
- R4: For NumGLUE-ds or NumGLUE-cm, drop the training row if normalised
  prompts are equal, or if their ordered sequences of numbers are equal.
  Use the audit's NFKC, casefold and whitespace normalisation, then the
  regular expression `\d+(?:\.\d+)?` to extract numbers. Empty sequences
  compare equal. Otherwise clear the pair as "template sibling with
  different quantities".
- R5: Independently of flags, drop MeetingBank training rows with an empty
  transcript: the prompt is exactly "Write a summary of the following meeting
  transcripts. Meeting transcripts: Summary:" after whitespace stripping,
  with nothing between "Meeting transcripts:" and "Summary:".

Assert identical normalised (prompt, answer) training contents receive the
same keep/drop verdict. Re-audit repaired training against unchanged eval,
test and exclusions; all remaining candidates need a cleared review bound
to this file's SHA256. Require at least 500 distinct normalised training
contents per subset task. Otherwise report PENDING and publish no final files.
